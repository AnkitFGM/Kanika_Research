Dissipative Particle Dynamics

DPDParticle simulationMesoscopic modelling

Dissipative Particle Dynamics (DPD) is a simulation approach that represents matter using interacting particles at a coarse-grained scale. It provides a way to explore how local interactions give rise to larger-scale behaviour.

Kanika’s specific research questions, model systems, and current projects will be added here once confirmed.

The method, in plain language

Instead of tracking every atom, a coarse-grained model groups microscopic detail into larger units. In DPD, particles interact through conservative, dissipative, and random forces.
